Butter Basted Steak

Description

A delicious steak recipe cooked with butter basting for enhanced flavor.

Ingredients

  • Steak (your preferred cut)
  • Butter
  • Salt and pepper
  • Fresh herbs (rosemary, thyme)

Instructions

  1. Heat a cast iron skillet over high heat
  2. Season steak with salt and pepper
  3. Sear steak on both sides until golden
  4. Add butter and herbs, baste continuously
  5. Cook to desired doneness
  6. Rest before serving
