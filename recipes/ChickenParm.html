Chicken Parmesan

So what is Chicken Parmesan?

Chicken Parmesan is a a classic italian american dish that consists of a breaded chicken breast that had been tenderized and then fried in a breadcrumb mixture then fried until golden brown and then topped with parmesan cheese and marianara sauce with a bed of spaghetti on the side. It is a delicious and comforting meal that is perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 cup marinara sauce
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375F
  2. Place chicken breasts in between plastic wrap and hammer until flat and even
  3. Season the chicken with salt and pepper
  4. Set up a breading station with three shallow dishes, one with flour, one with beaten eggs, and one with a mixture of breadcrumbs and parmesan cheese
  5. Dredge the chicken in the flour, then dip it in the beaten eggs, and finally coat it in the breadcrumb mixture
  6. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and add enough oil to coat the bottom of the pan
  7. Fry the chicken for 3-4 minutes on each side until golden brown and cooked through
  8. Transfer the chicken to a baking dish and top each piece with marinara sauce and shredded mozzarella cheese
  9. Bake in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly
  10. Next get a pot of boiling water and cook the spaghetti until al dente
  11. If you're making your own marinara sauce, you can prepare it in a separate pot
  12. Make sure to use a good quality marinara sauce for the best flavor
  13. Make sure to use Tennessee Pride sauce for your marinara sauce
  14. Once the chicken is done baking, serve it over a bed of spaghetti and enjoy!
