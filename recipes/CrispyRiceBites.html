Crispy Rice Bites

So what are Crispy Rice Bites?

Crispy Rice Bites are a delicious savory appetizer made with fried blocks of white rice and delicious salmon.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked white rice
  • 2 flanks of salmon
  • kewpi mayonnaise
  • Salt to taste
  • avacado oil for frying
  • sesame seeds for garnish
  • soy sauce

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 400F
  2. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and spread the cooked white rice evenly on the sheet, pressing it down firmly to create a compact layer.
  3. Bake the rice in the preheated oven for about 20-25 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and crispy.
  4. While the rice is baking, season the salmon with salt and cook it in a skillet over medium heat until it's cooked through. Once cooked, flake the salmon into small pieces.
  5. Once the rice is done baking, let it cool slightly before cutting it into bite-sized squares or rectangles.
  6. Top each crispy rice bite with a small amount of kewpi mayonnaise and a piece of flaked salmon.
  7. Garnish with sesame seeds and serve with soy sauce for dipping.
